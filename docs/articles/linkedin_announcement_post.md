Most AI safety tooling collapses moral reasoning to a number.

A scalar can tell you that "the soldier at the door asking where you're
hiding refugees" is "unsafe." It can't tell you which commitments are in
conflict, who bears imposed risk, or which authority is illegitimate.
The structure ethics is *about* gets thrown away at the output layer.

I just shipped Phase 4 of **ErisML Compiler** — a structure-preserving
compiler from natural-language moral material to a 10-dimensional moral
state, with a three-lens monitor that flags when a model's text output
and its internal activations disagree. End-to-end demo on Qwen2.5-7B
last week: the BIP equivariance check localised surface-form sensitivity
to the final layer exactly where the architecture predicts.
