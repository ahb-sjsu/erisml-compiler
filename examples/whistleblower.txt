As a financial analyst at the firm I owe loyalty to my employer and a duty of confidentiality to clients. I have benefited materially from this employment for several years.

Recently I have discovered that the institution is systematically misreporting risk to retail customers, exposing thousands of non-consenting members of the public to losses they were not warned about.

I must decide whether to remain silent in service of institutional loyalty, raise the issue through internal channels that have repeatedly ignored similar complaints, or disclose the wrongdoing to regulators and the affected public.
